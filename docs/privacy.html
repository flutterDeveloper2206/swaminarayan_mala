Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026
App: Swaminarayan Maala · Package: com.swaminarayanmalaa.app

This Privacy Policy explains how Swaminarayan Maala (“the App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use our mobile application on Android or iOS. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Summary

  • The App is designed to work offline.
  • Your jap counts, settings, and profile details are stored only on your device.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not show ads or sell your data.
  • We do not operate a cloud sync service for your sadhana data.

2. Information stored on your device

Depending on how you use the App, it may store locally:

  • Jap counts, mala progress, streaks, and session history
  • App settings (language, theme, sound, haptics, reminders, music preferences)
  • Optional profile name and profile photo you choose
  • Optional custom mantras and favourites you create
  • Optional ambient music file path if you import your own track

This information stays in on-device storage (for example Hive / app documents). It is not uploaded to our servers, because we do not collect it remotely.

3. Permissions we may use

  • Notifications — only if you enable jap reminders. You can turn reminders off in Settings.
  • Photos / media — only if you choose a profile photo or import an ambient music file. We do not upload these files.
  • Storage / files — only as needed to import or export data you initiate (for example backup / restore or music import).

We do not request access beyond what is needed for these optional features.

4. Analytics, advertising, and third parties

The core App does not include advertising SDKs or a requirement to use third-party analytics for jap counting. Your sadhana data is not sold or shared with third parties by us.

Your device platform (Google Play / Apple) may collect standard install and crash information under their own policies when you download or run apps from their stores. That collection is governed by Google or Apple, not by this App’s servers.

5. Children

The App includes an optional Kids Jap play mode intended for family use. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Kids Jap counts are stored on-device like other jap data. Parents/guardians should supervise device use as appropriate.

6. Data retention and deletion

Because data remains on your device, you control it. You can clear or reset local data using in-app options (Settings → Data) where available, or by clearing the App’s storage / uninstalling the App. Uninstalling removes local App data from that device.

7. Security

We rely on your device’s OS storage protections. Please keep your device locked and updated. No method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. International use

The App may be used wherever it is available. Processing of your jap data occurs locally on your device.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Effective date” above will change when we do. Continued use of the App after an update means you accept the revised policy.
